About STEM Forge

Building the next generation of engineers, one lesson at a time

🎯

Our Mission

STEM Forge empowers educators with ready-to-deploy, experiential robotics and engineering lessons that emphasize the Engineering Design Process (EDP) and hands-on learning.

⚙️

Engineering Design Process

Every lesson integrates EDP: Ask → Imagine → Plan → Create → Test & Improve. Students learn to iterate, fail forward, and think like real engineers.

🛡️

Safety First

All robotics and lab activities include detailed safety protocols, ensuring a secure learning environment for secondary school students.

📚

Curriculum Alignment

Designed for grades 8-12, aligned with NGSS and CSTA standards. Each lesson includes learning objectives, timelines, and assessment prompts.

✨ What Makes STEM Forge Unique

✓ Experiential learning activities that mirror real-world engineering challenges
✓ Robotics-focused curriculum with Arduino, sensors, and autonomous systems
✓ Cross-disciplinary lessons (Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science)
✓ Interactive timeline and activity preview for just-in-time teaching support
✓ Engineering Design Process explicitly embedded in every lesson phase
